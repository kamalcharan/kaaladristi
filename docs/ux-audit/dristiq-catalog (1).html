EMA 20

✦
VaNi explains
cached · updated rarely
EMA 20 gives more weight to the last 20 sessions than older data, so it reacts faster to price changes than a simple moving average. Think of it as a short-term memory of the market — it tells you the recent direction without the noise of individual sessions. When price crosses above EMA 20, the short-term tide has turned. When it holds below, the recent momentum is down.
